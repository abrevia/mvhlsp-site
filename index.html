Terms of Service & Neutral Conduit Doctrine

1. Neutral Linguistic Conduit Doctrine

MVHLSP and its independent interpreting collective operate strictly as verbatim linguistic transmission conduits under Section 1557 ACA and HIPAA Privacy standards. Interpreters do not practice medicine, render clinical diagnoses, or evaluate medical necessity. All medical decisions and care remain under the non-delegable authority of the attending licensed healthcare provider.

2. Zero-Data-Trace (ZDT) Operational Architecture

Encounters facilitated via MVHLSP use encrypted real-time telecommunications (SIP-TLS, SRTP, and WebRTC). In accordance with HHS HIPAA conduit exceptions, transient spoken and visual protected health information (PHI) is never permanently stored, recorded, transcribed, or retained on intermediary servers.

3. Transparent Per-Second Metering

Services are rendered on-demand at $1.48 USD per minute ($0.0247 USD per second) with zero monthly minimum commitments, zero retainer fees, and zero locked contracts. Metering begins upon active interpreter connection and concludes immediately upon session disconnection.

4. Professional Ethics & Standards

All interpreters operate in strict alignment with National Council on Interpreting in Health Care (NCIHC) National Code of Ethics and ASTM F2089 standards for healthcare interpreting.
